Adds independent kitchen preparation orders from the native Waiter app. Requests contain selected items and a hall; prices, waiter identity and station routing come from the server. Each station gets a separate Bengali slip on the responsible Counter PC, including a hall's second PC.

Kitchen slips remain outside sales, bill numbering, payments and cash-day records. Exact retries remain unique, and the station print transport stays quiet during polling. Owner/Admin sees these slips in the existing read-only Kitchen orders page. Requires Waiter 1.0.23-simple; works with Counter 1.0.43.
